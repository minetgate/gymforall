Privacy Policy Effective November 20, 2026 Summary Gym4All is published by Pablo Mendez. This policy explains what information the app handles and who receives it. The short version: what you enter in the app stays on your device. We do not run a server that stores it, and you do not create an account with us. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells an optional ad-free subscription through the App Store or Google Play, managed with RevenueCat; those services receive the limited information described below. Information you enter You can enter a name, age, sex, weight, height, training level, goals, injuries or sore areas, equipment, the workouts you complete, body-weight entries, an optional body-fat percentage, your training days and check-ins, and food and unit preferences. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us, and the app does not share it with advertising partners. It is used on the device to build your plan, estimate muscle mass, calculate nutrition targets and show your progress. Progress photos and the camera You can add a "before" and an "after" photo to a plan. The app asks for the camera only when you choose to take a photo, and opens the photo picker only when you choose an existing one. The photos are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded, not shared with us or with advertising partners, and not added to your photo gallery. You can delete each photo in the app, and deleting your data or uninstalling the app removes them all. Workout reminders If you turn reminders on, the app asks for permission to show notifications and your phone schedules a reminder on your training days. The reminders are created on the device: we do not send push notifications and no notification data leaves your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings at any time. Advertising (Google AdMob) Unless you have the ad-free subscription, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which indicates approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and on iOS it asks permission before using the advertising identifier. You can change your choice at any time under Profile > Remove ads > Ad privacy choices where that option applies, and in your device's privacy settings. If the age in your profile is under 18, the app asks Google to treat ad requests as coming from a user under the age of consent and to show only content suitable for general audiences. Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ad-free subscription (App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat) Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Exercise clips and pictures The app's exercise clips, exercise pictures and food pictures are stored inside the app. Showing them sends no data to anyone. What we do not do We do not sell your personal information. We do not run analytics or tracking of our own. We do not upload your profile, health-related entries or workout history anywhere. Deleting your data Profile > Delete my account and data permanently erases your profile, plans, workout history, body-weight log and settings from the device and resets your ad consent choice. Uninstalling the app has the same effect on the data stored by the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing further for us to delete. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. For information held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact us and we will help direct your request. Children The app is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by them. Body-composition and nutrition estimates are not shown to users under 18. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these directly in the app and in your device settings, as described above. You can also contact us. Changes If this policy changes, the new version will be available in the app and the effective date above will be updated. Contact Pablo Mendez — support@longwavesolutions.com
