Gym4All Support Gym4All is published by Pablo Mendez. Contact Write to support@longwavesolutions.com. Please say whether you use an iPhone or an Android phone and what you were doing when the problem appeared. Removing ads, and restoring a purchase Open Profile, then Remove ads. You can pay by the month, by the year, or once for life. All three remove every ad and unlock nothing else. If you reinstall the app or change phone, open the same screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account. Cancelling a subscription Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by the app. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Ads return when the period you paid for ends. Refunds Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We cannot issue them ourselves. Your data Gym4All has no account. Your profile, workouts, body-weight entries and progress photos are stored only on your phone. To erase them, open Profile and choose Delete my account and data. Uninstalling the app also removes them. A new phone starts empty, because nothing is kept on a server. Workout reminders Choose your training days and a time in Profile, then turn reminders on. If none arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Gym4All in your phone's settings. On Android a reminder can arrive a few minutes late. Health Gym4All gives general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new programme if you have an injury or a health condition, and stop any exercise that causes pain.
